## 1. INTRODUCTION

Signal Arena ("we", "us", "the Platform") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

---

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Email address** (optional, for account recovery)
- **Username** (in-game display name)
- **Wallet address** (public blockchain address, if you connect a wallet)
- **Payment information** (processed by Stripe, we do not store card details)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Gameplay data**: battles, scores, XP, skill ratings, entity encounters
- **Device information**: browser type, OS, screen resolution
- **IP address**: for security and rate limiting
- **Cookies**: for session management and preferences
- **Analytics**: page views, session duration (anonymized)

### 2.3 On-Chain Data
- **Wallet address**: public blockchain information
- **Transaction history**: $SIG token transactions (public on blockchain)
- **Staking data**: stake amount, duration, rewards

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict** processing
- **Data portability** (export your data)
- **Object** to processing
- **Withdraw consent** at any time

**Note**: On-chain data (blockchain transactions) cannot be deleted due to the immutable nature of blockchain technology.

To exercise your rights: privacy@signalarena.io

## 7. SECURITY

We implement industry-standard security:

- TLS/SSL encryption for all communications
- Password hashing (bcrypt)
- Rate limiting and DDoS protection
- Regular security audits
- Multi-signature treasury
- Principle of least privilege for staff access
