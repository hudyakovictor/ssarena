## 🎮 THE GAME

### Concept

Every mistake traders make in real markets is personified as a **Market Entity** — a creature with unique psychology, behavior patterns, and counter-strategies. Players face these entities in time-pressured battles where they must:

1. **Analyze** real market data (charts, on-chain, sentiment, news)
2. **Allocate** limited Attention Points (AP) to data sources
3. **Decide** the correct action under pressure
4. **Learn** from Terminal Voice feedback (layered analysis)
